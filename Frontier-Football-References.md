## The tension, spelled out

This is the part worth arguing about, so I'm putting it first.

Our brand guide is bright. Frontier Yellow as the hero colour, a cream base, hand drawn doodles of boots and bottles and gloves and whistles, paint splat textures, a scarf and crest badge, photo strip framing. Warm, playful, grassroots. Sri Lanka's football community, and the line that sums it up: talent was never missing, the platform was.

Ten of the fourteen references go the other way. Near black backgrounds, cinematic photography, restrained editorial type, cold neutrals.

That doesn't make them bad references. It means they're useful for one thing and not another. Use them for layout systems, component patterns, scroll behaviour, how to structure a long page. Don't use them to decide what the site feels like. Lazarev and the HOOPS shot are the only two already sitting near our energy, so treat those as the tonal anchors.

## 1. How it works: layout and UI

### [OFFSIDE, Football Community Landing Page](https://dribbble.com/shots/25756205-OFFSIDE-Football-Community-Landing-Page)
Royhan, for One Week Wonders

Editorial magazine layout on off white. Huge black condensed caps. The league rows (Bundesliga, Premier League, Serie A, La Liga) are hairline ruled list items with a VIEW MORE on each. Then the "Football Area" section flips to dark cards: a player stat card showing 22 goals, 2 assists and 28% goal conversion as a segmented bar, a player story card, a club standing card, carousel arrows. The transfer news block mixes black and pale blue in one headline and uses likelihood bars (Chelsea 85%, Napoli 72%, PSG 51%). The footer is one enormous HELLO@OFFSIDE.COM.

**What to take:** the league row list, the stat card grid, and percentage bars as a visual device. This is the closest structural match we have.

### [Sports Website UI, "HOOPS"](https://dribbble.com/shots/20327829-Sports-Website-UI)
Bogdan Falin, for QClay

Warm light grey base with a burnt orange accent. The hero sets giant outlined BALL letterforms and fills them with a grid of photo tiles, so the type doubles as a photo mask. Black pill nav. Underneath there's a three step onboarding strip: a black card with a minus/plus counter for how many teammates you need, an orange card with a radio list for indoor, street or complex, and a white card for location access.

**What to take:** the type as photo mask trick is cheap and hits hard. The three step strip is a good model for a "join a match" flow.

### [Sports app design concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/21356151-Sports-app-design-concept-Lazarev)
Lazarev, UI/UX Design Agency

Light and bright. A massive black condensed headline, CHASE YOUR DREAM WITH ALL YOUR MIGHT, with photos and 3D objects tucked inside and between the letters. A row of saturated program cards along the bottom in orange, purple and lime, each with a black play button.

**What to take:** honestly, the whole attitude. It's warm, bright and unprecious, which is where we actually live. That multi colour card row maps almost straight onto our palette.

## 2. How it feels: tone and product patterns

### [Tenista, Sport Community, Trainer, Facilities](https://dribbble.com/shots/26020692-Tenista-Sport-Community-Trainer-Facilities-for-Tennis)
Sans Design, for Sans Brothers

Warm cream base, olive green photography, deep green cards. Rounded stat pills float over the imagery: 18K+ with 12K+ membership, 3.2k impressions, 100% train with the best, #01 highly trusted. The hero is a full bleed racket and balls shot with GAME ON, PLAY HARD in white caps. Facilities run as rounded photo tiles.

**What to take:** a warm cream base with one strong colour on top, which is the same logic as our cream plus green. The floating social proof pills would work well on a community platform.

### [Louvre Sports Center, landing page](https://dribbble.com/shots/25148418-Louvre-sports-center-landing-page)
Tosan Garditama, for One Week Wonders

Top down court photography as the hero with a white italic headline over it. Below that, a facilities explorer built from rounded photo tiles, a filter pill row by sport, a testimonial block with a big quote mark, and a blue events and newsletter CTA card.

**What to take:** the booking and facilities half of the product, already solved. Filter pills plus a card explorer is close to what a venues or fixtures page needs.

### [Live Football App](https://dribbble.com/shots/25540442-Live-Football-App)
DIGI.CO

Near black app UI presented on pitch green. The match report hero has a full bleed photo, a duration pill and a green PLAY button. Match schedule runs as clean list rows, club badge then score then club badge, separated by hairlines with date and time above. News cards put a thumbnail left and headline right. The detail screen has a green WATCH NOW, engagement counts, and a Live grid with LIVE badges.

**What to take:** that match schedule row. Of everything in this set it's the single most directly reusable component we have.

### [Sport News Mobile App](https://dribbble.com/shots/25557189-Sport-News-Mobile-App)
DIGI.CO

Same studio. Deep crimson presentation background, near black UI, red accent everywhere. Heavy condensed uppercase headlines. Tabs across News, Sport, Formula1, Rugby, Tennis. Red LIVE badges, red WATCH VIDEO and READ ALL buttons, engagement rows, an EXPLORE MORE thumbnail grid and a RELATED CONTENT list.

**What to take:** the crimson here is nearly identical to our `#87292D`. It's a working demo that our red can hold a whole dark interface together, which is handy if we ever build a dark mode.

## 3. Live sites, for storytelling

All seven are live and I read them directly. Use them for narrative structure and ambition. Their palettes are not for us.

**[United in Football](https://united-in-football.framer.website/)** is a cinematic 2026 World Cup tribute. Full screen scroll chapters, high contrast photography, big headline type with a lot of air, an interactive winners table, and sections with audio of fans reacting. It uses real numbers (5 billion fans, 211+ nations) as emotional beats rather than as stats, which is a trick worth stealing.

**[The Immortals](https://theimmortals.world/)** is a World Cup archive from 1930 to 2026, designed by Jason Jerez. A dark grid of tournament year cards, each with a poetic title over an archival image. 1986 is "The Hand and the Crown", 1954 is "The Miracle".

**[Last Dance for Glory](https://lastdanceforglory.world/)** is gold on near black, structured as chapters: The Legends, The Contenders, The Underdogs, The Heirs, Trophy Remembers. I'd use it mostly as an upper bound. It shows how far you can push editorial drama before a site stops being usable.

**[BVB Tribute](https://www.bvb-tribute.com/)** covers Dortmund's Klopp years, 2010 to 2013, season by season, with player profiles and fan testimonials running through it. The testimonial format would transfer well to us.

**[No Football Colors](https://nofootballcolors.com/)** is an independent football culture and design brand. Minimal white and grey, spacious grid, an editorial stories hub, a shop, a newsletter. "No games. Just football through the eyes of design." Proof that a football brand can be quiet and still feel real to the culture.

**[Player Album](https://playeralbum.com/)** is an interactive digital World Cup 2026 sticker album. 110 collectible cards, completion tracking, sharing a finished album. If we ever want a collectible hook, this is the model.

**[Ballerleague UK](https://ballerleague.uk/en)** is the six a side league site. Fully dark, card grid for results, league table, stats, teams, players, shop, news. It reads as sports entertainment rather than sports federation, which is closer to our register than most of this list.

## Open questions

1. Light or dark? The brand guide says light. Most of these references say dark. Whichever we pick decides most of what follows.
2. Are we building a fixtures and results layer? If so, start from the Live Football App row and the OFFSIDE league rows.
3. Do we want a collectible or gamified hook? Player Album is the reference if we do.
4. How hard do we lean on the doodle pattern and paint splat textures? They're the most distinctive thing we own, and nothing in this reference set uses anything like them.

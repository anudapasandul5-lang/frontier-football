Same word, same size, three free Google-Fonts candidates for the giant condensed caps.

FRONTIER

FRONTIER

Bebas Neue

Recommendation: Anton. It is the only one of the three that is both genuinely condensed AND a true black/heavy weight at once — Archivo Black is bold but full-width, not condensed; Bebas Neue is condensed but a light single weight that thins out at crushed 0.8 line-height. Anton holds up as a solid sticker-like mass at 200–640px, closest to the brand guide's Super Squad and to the Lazarev reference's giant black caps. Body face: Space Grotesk, per brief, no argument for swapping it.

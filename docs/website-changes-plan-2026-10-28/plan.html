Photo rules

The thin lines are the camera grid you see when taking a photo. The red dot is the dead centre: the main face goes right on it. Same rules for every photo on the site.

1 · Sharp only
✓ Sharp, in focus
✗ Blurry or soft

Only in-focus photos. If a better, sharper photo of the same moment exists, we use that one.

2 · No empty space
✓ Cropped tight, people fill it
✗ Big empty space left in

Crop out blank space around the people, so the photo is about them and not the floor, wall or sky.

3 · Centre the faces
✓ Main face in the dead centre
✗ Face pushed to the side

The main face sits on the centre of the grid. Today some photos, like Ground Zero, sit off to one side.

4 · Nothing cut off
✓ Every head fully inside
✗ Head or body cut off

Every head and face stays fully inside the lines. We never crop through a person.

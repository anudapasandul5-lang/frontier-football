## Lead verification and usable evidence

The lead reopened the strongest research claims on 26 October. The FIFA page returned no body; the UEFA PDF exceeded the web tool's size limit. Neither is accepted as a verified visual reference. Their entries below remain discovery notes only. Search-derived descriptions are not visual evidence.

- Existing United reference, [The Last Dance capture](../design/united/.firecrawl/section-last-dance.png), visually inspected by the lead this session. Observed: dark canvas, huge condensed type, hairline/tick rules, small labels and much larger editorial photos. Adapt its image/type scale relationship while making Frontier's actual champion name the focal point. Do not copy its Ronaldo imagery, copy, or long pinned choreography. This is a saved historical capture, not a live-site verification.
- [NN/g: Photos as Web Content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/), opened and read by the lead. Supports relevant real-person imagery rather than decorative filler. It does not prove a larger Frontier photo will improve a measured outcome.
- [NN/g: Visual Design Principles](https://media.nngroup.com/media/articles/attachments/Principles_Visual_Design-A4.pdf), opened and extracted by the lead. Supports using relative scale and colour to signal importance. The recommendation is our design inference, not a tested Frontier result.
- [W3C: Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html), opened and read by the lead. Supports disabling nonessential interaction motion and keeping the static experience complete.

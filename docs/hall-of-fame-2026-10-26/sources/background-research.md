# Active background references

Goal: add atmosphere behind the existing Hall of Fame rows without changing their order, content, or layout.

## Three useful precedents

- [UEFA, “Kick of Light” identity](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0290-1badd36e73b2-c3e5755b7fae-1000--uefa-unveils-new-uefa-champions-league-brand-identity/) — **Observed in UEFA's description:** starball/stadium dome made of glass, with color moving along star edges; article image link fetch failed, so I did not inspect the artwork itself. **Adapt:** low-contrast stadium-arc linework with one slow yellow light pass. **Avoid:** copied starball, prism rainbow, or bright full-screen glow.
- [Rishab Soni, “Trophy in Motion”](https://www.rishabsoni.com/home/uefa) — **Observed in creator portfolio text:** five short pitch stings center on a trophy silhouette, revealed through quick macro shots; the client concept did not proceed. **Adapt:** one oversized, very faint trophy outline as a fixed background watermark behind the rows. **Avoid:** rapid cuts, metallic 3D, or suggesting Frontier has an official trophy.
- [Tektura Studio, Polish Cup Director’s Cut](https://www.tektura.studio/work/sts-puchar-polski) — **Observed in studio description:** trophy close-ups, reflections, archival textures and broadcast overlays build tension. Creator image links did not load here. **Adapt:** restrained grain or thin replay-like line trails to add depth. **Avoid:** distressed texture that lowers text contrast.

## Direction for this page

Keep every existing result row intact. Put a dark, low-opacity stadium outline behind the whole section; let a small yellow light sweep travel once on load, then stop. Keep photos and champion names dominant. On small screens, reduce or hide the outline. Disable motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`. This is an inference from the references and Frontier’s current dark/yellow palette, not a tested design.

## Cleanup list (no layout change), biggest win first

1. Home Moment: cut the floating thumbnails to 2 or 3 on laptop and none on phone. This is the main "messy" moment.
2. ALL CAPS only for headlines and buttons. Small labels ("AS OF SEPTEMBER 2026", "PHOTOS: THEPAPARE", eyebrows) go sentence case.
3. Smallest text 12px. Cut font sizes to one scale of about 6 steps.
4. Hall of Fame laptop: line up the "Champion" rule and photo at the same height in every card. Phone: fade the trophy drawing well behind the text, and keep Pause clear of it.
5. Home: shorten the empty dark scroll after Who We Are and before Milestones.
6. Fewer photos per section: one lead photo, max 2 to 3 support photos (Gallery, Community, Who We Are timeline).
7. Inner page headlines: start the slide-in at once instead of after about 1.3 s.

Not checked: real phone, screen reader, production build speed. The Next "N" dev badge only shows in dev.

# Pitch presence

The website is the subject. Its typography, photographs and crest carry the identity; the surrounding presentation gives them space to read. Meticulously aligned screens and generous margins turn a busy football archive into a clear visual sequence.

Bone, ink and Frontier yellow form the stage. Solid fields retain the site's character. Colour is used with restraint, with painstaking attention to the balance between the page and its frame.

Desktop and phone proportions establish rhythm. A wide view introduces the platform, a closer view reveals the events, and the phone demonstrates the responsive experience. Master-level execution here means preserving the real screen content without invented interface elements.

Text is sparse. A small project label anchors the scene; the website supplies the story. Crisp edges, careful spacing and consistent screen ratios keep every composition focused on the work.
